A FRESH CHAT. THE SAME WORKSPACE.

Let a chat set the starting point.

In any chat, ask it to read AGENT.md in your Waypoint installation folder (use its full path), then give it your materials and describe the work you want to track.

“Configure Waypoint for revising my slides and script. This belongs to [course/project]. Read the materials and make a checklist of the pages that need my attention. Keep a link to this chat.”

The guide explains how to create a task configuration in your data folder. You can also import a task JSON file using the sidebar.

The chat prepares the plan. You mark your progress here. Completion dates, focus time, and notes stay together in your data folder.
